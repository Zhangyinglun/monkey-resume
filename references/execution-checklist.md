# Completion and failure checks

## Cache-only work

- Original bytes and located text preserved outside the Skill repository.
- Model proposal checked against full source meaning; no fabricated unknown fields.
- Stable candidate/entity IDs reused, project ownership and metric scope preserved.
- Current context checked before atomic commit; duplicate imports remain no-ops.
- Corrections and conflicts retain history; both sides of unresolved conflicts are
  withheld from support. Shorter source omissions do not erase earlier facts.
- Report actual paths/counts/conflicts and important unparsed content. Source
  preservation or transaction success alone is not full semantic coverage.

## Resume preparation

- Literal original JD and known target metadata retained.
- Full active evidence and latest supported display name read.
- Required facts in `resume-working-schema.md` complete and evidence-bound for
  every selected entry; unresolved required facts block preparation.
- Complete draft rewritten for the JD, with supported skills and important omission
  reasons, bound to the current context; no additional plan/language files.
- Open-source and patent content selected only after applying the inclusion
  criteria in `optional-modules.md`; space assigned only to included contributions.
- All five required rules in `resume-language-quality.md` checked against prose
  and evidence; concise presentation preserves actual capability and metric scope.
- Every experience/project bullet's opening verb checked under rule 4; review
  records locations, repeated groups and corrections or reasons for retaining them.
- Traceability checks pass without being described as semantic proof.
- The current `professional-v3` style is recorded and uses Arial. Required fonts
  are available and actual rendered text uses that family without substitution.
- Actual candidate DOCX/PDF remain unaccepted until unified review completes.

## Follow-up revisions

- Corresponding version, original JD and editable draft recovered under
  `resume-revisions.md`; frozen and delivered files remain intact until publication.
- Exact user request committed as scoped feedback in the existing candidate
  preferences and re-read; factual corrections also use the evidence Ledger.
- Current context reconciled and draft rebound after feedback commits; requested
  changes applied without unrelated rewriting or loss of prior preferences.
- New candidate receives current independent content and actual visual review;
  a previous acceptance or wording approval does not approve the revised file.
- Handoff states the change, saved preference scope and actual acceptance status.

## Review and publication

- Independent content review uses original JD and complete source evidence, checks
  both assertions and important omissions, and gives concrete findings.
- Required facts checked against sources and actual visible output under
  `recruiter-review-protocol.md`; omissions are blocking.
- All seven `evidence_review.fact_review` sections contain passing source-based
  accuracy/completeness results and cover every listed display path. Whole
  education/employment records and individual contact components reconciled;
  optional omissions explained without forcing private or irrelevant information.
- Supplementary value of included open-source and patent content independently
  checked and recorded under `recruiter-review-protocol.md`.
- Exact candidate DOCX opened directly in a document reader's paginated view;
  every page inspected and reader name/version plus observations recorded.
  PDF-derived previews do not establish direct DOCX inspection. Inspect all PDF
  pages additionally when delivering PDF. Internal PDF measurements still pass.
- Contact row occupies exactly one rendered line in the direct DOCX view (and
  any delivered PDF); measured
  `contact_single_line` QA passes and observed counts are recorded in the review.
- Every bullet ending verified against the required rendered-width rule, including
  single-line bullets; physical QA alone does not certify this check.
- Review comes from the actual review context, not writer-generated PASS; ordinary
  suggestions do not mask unresolved blocking findings.
- Candidate, draft, context and exact artifacts match the review bindings.
- Publication rechecks font evidence against the frozen style; a failed candidate
  preserves previous Accepted Resumes.
- Direct DOCX reader/version/results are reported separately from automated PDF
  measurements; unavailable reader checks are not reported as passes.
- Delivery is DOCX by default; PDF handoff requires an explicit request or entrance
  requirement. Same-source QA PDF and accepted history remain retained internally.
- Only then publish; source, original candidate, prior Accepted files and history
  remain intact on handled failures. Handoff paths and true limitations reported.

Unavailable independent review, conversion, fonts or visual inspection is an
unverified candidate, not a successful Accepted Resume. Repeated disputes without
new progress or one-page/must-keep conflicts require a specific user choice.
